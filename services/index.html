Review

An independent look at the Power BI reporting you already have, before anyone commits to fixing or rebuilding it. I go through it layer by layer: the semantic model and its DAX, the queries and loads underneath, and the manual steps that feed it. 02

Build and rebuild

Covers
Power BI DAX Power Query T-SQL Report design Power BI Service

New Power BI reporting built from the data up, or existing reporting rebuilt so people trust it again. The work runs from the sources and the SQL layer, through the semantic model and DAX, to the report pages, then on to deployment, refresh and access on the Power BI Service. The aim is figures that are defensible, not just presentable. Where manual steps feed the reporting, Power Automate and SharePoint can take them over.

What's included

  • Semantic modelling, star-schema design and DAX, documented measure by measure
  • T-SQL and Power Query work under the model, so logic sits in the right layer
  • Report pages designed around the questions each audience needs answered
  • Every figure reconciled against its source before go-live
  • Deployment, workspaces, refresh and row-level security
  • Source files in your own repository, with notes your team can follow
When this fits

You're setting up reporting for the first time and want it built to last. Or the reporting you have is slow, inconsistent or simply not trusted.

How I work

How the work runs.

The steps stay the same; how deep each one goes depends on the job. When I join an existing team, I work its way and bring the checks with me.

  1. 01

    Understand what it's for

    Who reads the report, what they decide with it, and which numbers have to be right. If no decision follows from a number, it doesn't earn a place on the page.

  2. 02

    Check the data first

    Before modelling anything, I profile the sources: what one row means, where the gaps are, what doesn't join. Problems found at this stage are the cheapest to fix.

  3. 03

    Build, and show it early

    The model comes first, then the report. You see it while it takes shape, so feedback arrives while it's still easy to act on.

  4. 04

    Reconcile and hand over

    Every figure is checked against its source before go-live. The files, and the notes on how it all works, stay with you, so your team can run it without me.
