About

An engineer's approach to data.

I'm Lorenzo Reale, an independent Power BI consultant. I trained as an aerospace engineer, then spent six years in London, where I built enterprise reporting that operational teams and leadership relied on.

I did that work in large, highly regulated organisations, where every figure has to stand up to audit and scrutiny. Earlier in my career I ran a retail store end to end, from campaigns and sales performance to the layout of the shop floor. Having been on both sides of the numbers, I build reporting that fits the way decisions actually get made.

I'm a British and Italian citizen, so I can work in the UK and the EU without visas or sponsorship. Most of my work is delivered remotely, and I'm open to occasional on-site visits when a project needs them.

Approach

Good reporting starts long before the dashboard.

Most reporting problems aren't reporting problems. When operational numbers don't reconcile, the cause is usually upstream, in how the data was captured, modelled and moved, long before it ever reached Power BI.

I work with operational data, where the KPIs and the figures leadership acts on have to be defensible, not just presentable. I've built reporting in demanding, high-stakes environments, so I start at the data layer, not the chart.

That means semantic models that hold their logic, DAX you can audit, and a SQL foundation that won't quietly drift. The dashboard comes last, and it is only as trustworthy as what sits beneath it.
