Approach

Good reporting starts long before the dashboard.

Most reporting problems aren't reporting problems. When operational numbers don't reconcile, the cause is usually upstream, in how the data was captured, modelled and moved, long before it ever reached Power BI.

So I start at the data layer. I work in regulated utilities, gas distribution and infrastructure delivery, where operational KPIs and compliance figures have to be defensible, not just presentable.

That means semantic models that hold their logic, DAX you can audit, and a SQL foundation that won't quietly drift. Building the dashboard is the easy part, and the last.
