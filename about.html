My Journey

I didn't start as a traditional Computer Science grad. My entry into tech was through data.

After an intensive residency at iO-Sphere, I joined Costain as a Data Quality Engineer. I spent my days in the trenches of operational data—fixing dashboards, writing SQL queries, and understanding how data drives physical work in the energy sector.

But I kept running into a problem: Reporting on bad data is painful. I wanted to fix the source.

That curiosity pushed me into the backend. I started learning C#, .NET, and Azure architecture to build the tools that capture data correctly in the first place. Now, I operate as a hybrid engineer—building the APIs that feed the dashboards I used to design.
